ABOUT ME

Turning information into stories people remember.

Communication is more than sharing information. It is about understanding people, identifying what matters and presenting it in a way that creates understanding and connection.

As a Communication Officer, I work across storytelling, photography, digital content, documentation and communication materials. My work focuses on transforming field experiences and project activities into clear, engaging and human-centered stories.

I enjoy working with people, visiting project sites, documenting real experiences and developing content that communicates both the human story and the impact behind a project.
